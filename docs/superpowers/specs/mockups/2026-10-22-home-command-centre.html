Command centre: which look?

Same layout, three styles, at the frame's real 1280×800 size (scaled down). The calendar now has hour lines, a divider between days, a red "now" line, and greyed-out past events. Click the style you like; mixing is fine ("night colours, but paper fonts").

1 · Warm paper

Closest to Wes's: cream background, soft cards with no borders, serif headings. Homely, and suits a living space.

2 · Night

A dark theme with less glare in an office or in the evening, and it hides the frame's bezel. Could switch to this automatically after sunset.

3 · Clean grid

Crisp white with thin borders, modelled on Notion Calendar. The most "tool-like" of the three, with the strongest lines.
